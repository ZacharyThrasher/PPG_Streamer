# Movie night: getting connected (5 minutes, one time)

This page covers the connection part only (Tailscale). The player install and movie-night steps are in the main friend guide: [../friends/FRIEND-GUIDE.md](../friends/FRIEND-GUIDE.md).

## You need

- A Windows PC, Mac or Linux computer
- A Google, Microsoft, Apple or GitHub account (any one; free)
- The invite link from Zach
- Your Jellyfin username and password from Zach

## Steps

1. **Install Tailscale.** Get it from https://tailscale.com/download (Windows, Mac, Linux). Open it and sign in with your Google/Microsoft/Apple/GitHub account.
   - Tailscale is a small private-network app. It does not slow down your normal internet.
2. **Click the invite link Zach sent you.** Sign in if asked, then press **Accept**.
   - You should see Zach's PC appear in your Tailscale device list.
3. **Keep the address Zach sent.** It looks like `https://something.tailnet-name.ts.net`. You type it into the Jellyfin player in the next guide.
4. **Continue with the player setup** in [../friends/FRIEND-GUIDE.md](../friends/FRIEND-GUIDE.md) (install the Jellyfin player, enter the address, log in).

If the player will not install on your computer, you can open the same address in Chrome or Edge and log in there instead.

## If something does not work

| What you see | Try this |
|---|---|
| Player or page cannot reach the server | Open the Tailscale app and make sure it says Connected. Then reopen the player. |
| Step 2 says the invite is used or expired | Ask Zach for a new link. Each one works once and expires after 30 days. |
| Video is choppy | Tell Zach. It may be using a slow route and he can check. Close other heavy downloads or streams. |
| Smart TV or streaming box | Synced nights do not work on TVs yet. Use a laptop, or plug a laptop into the TV with an HDMI cable. |

Still stuck? Send Zach a screenshot of the Tailscale app and the error page.
