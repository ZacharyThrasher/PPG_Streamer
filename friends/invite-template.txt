**Movie night setup - about 5 minutes, one time**

[[tailscale]]
**1. Connect.** Install Tailscale from https://tailscale.com/download and sign in with Google, Microsoft, Apple or GitHub. Then open this link and press Accept: {INVITE_LINK}

[[/tailscale]]
**Install the player.** It is free and called Jellyfin Media Player.
Windows: press the Windows key, type PowerShell, open it, paste this line and press Enter:
`winget install Jellyfin.JellyfinMediaPlayer`
Mac, Linux, or winget does not work: download it from https://github.com/jellyfin/jellyfin-desktop/releases/latest (pick the file for your computer).

**Log in.** Open Jellyfin Media Player. When it asks for a server, type:
`{URL}`
Username: `{USER}`
Password: `{PASSWORD}`
Leave every setting alone. You are done.

**On movie night:** join the call, open the player, wait until I say the group is ready, then click the people icon at the top right and press my group's name. When I press play, the movie starts for you by itself. Anyone's pause pauses everyone, so only pause for a real break and say so on voice. Do not drag the seek bar.

No smart TVs for synced nights yet. Use a laptop or PC. Stuck? Screenshot it and send it to me.
